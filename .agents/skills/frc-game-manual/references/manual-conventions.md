# FRC Manual Conventions and Terminology

To accurately interpret the FRC Game Manual, understand its structure and vocabulary before quoting rules.

## Rule Categories

Rules are prefixed with a letter indicating their section:

- `(G)` Game Rules: on-field gameplay, safety, and conduct during matches, such as `G408`.
- `(R)` Robot Construction Rules: robot design, weight, bumpers, motors, and electronics, such as `R103`.
- `(I)` Inspection and Eligibility: inspection process and robot modification rules.
- `(T)` Tournaments: match schedules, replays, playoff brackets, awards, and advancement.
- `(C)` FIRST Championship: rules specific to FIRST Championship events.
- `(E)` Event Rules: pits, load-in, machine shops, stands, and venue behavior.
- `(Q)` Question Box: rules for interactions with the Head Referee at an event.

## Rule Formatting

- Evergreen rules marked with `*` retain a stable core intent across seasons, but game-specific terms can still change. Verify the current year's text.
- Headlines summarize rules but do not supersede rule text.
- Blue boxes provide examples, intent, and best practices. They are not binding rule text.
- All-caps terms are defined terms from the glossary, such as `STARTING CONFIGURATION`, `CONTROL`, `BUMPER`, and `MATCH`.
- Tables often carry binding numeric values. Cite the table name or number when using a value.

## Penalties

- `MINOR FOUL`: 5 points credited to the opponent's score.
- `MAJOR FOUL`: 15 points credited to the opponent's score.
- `YELLOW CARD`: formal warning for egregious behavior. A second yellow card in the same tournament phase becomes a red card.
- `RED CARD`: disqualification for the match. In playoffs, a red card can apply to the whole alliance.
- `DISABLED`: the robot is commanded by the Field Management System to deactivate outputs for the rest of the match.

## Timing Vocabulary

- `MOMENTARY`: fewer than approximately 3 seconds.
- `CONTINUOUS`: more than approximately 10 seconds.
- `REPEATED`: happening more than once within a match.

## Interpretation Discipline

- Quote the rule text before interpreting edge cases.
- Check whether a Team Update changed the section being cited.
- Check Official Q&A for official clarifications when wording is ambiguous.
- Avoid treating quick references, blog posts, event anecdotes, or historical habits as authority.
