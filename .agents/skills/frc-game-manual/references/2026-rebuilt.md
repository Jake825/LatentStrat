# 2026 Game: REBUILT Presented by Haas

This is a quick-reference guide, not a legality source. For rule answers, verify exact wording in `assets/manuals/2026GameManual-TU22.pdf`, check `assets/manuals/REBUILT_TeamUpdate-Combined.pdf`, and consult the current Official Q&A when needed.

## Game Elements

- `FUEL`: the scoring element, a 5.91 in (15.0 cm) diameter high-density yellow foam ball.
- `HUB`: a 47 x 47 in central scoring structure. The top opening is hexagonal (41.7 in) and 72 in off the carpet. FUEL scored here is distributed back into the neutral zone.
- `TOWER`: a climbing structure integrated into the alliance wall containing a low rung (27 in), mid rung (45 in), and high rung (63 in).
- `BUMP`: 6.5 in tall, 15-degree ramps on either side of the HUB.
- `TRENCH`: a structure robots drive underneath (40.25 in tall arm, 50.34 in wide, 22.25 in clearance).
- `OUTPOST` and `DEPOT`: areas where human players can introduce FUEL to the field.

## Match Periods and Shifts

The 2026 game features an alternating-goal mechanic during the teleoperated period.

- `AUTO` (0:20 - 0:00): 20 seconds. Robots operate autonomously. Both HUBS are active.
- `TRANSITION SHIFT` (2:20 - 2:10): 10 seconds. Drivers take control. Both HUBS are active.
- `SHIFT 1` (2:10 - 1:45): 25 seconds. Only one HUB is active. The alliance that scored the most FUEL in AUTO has their HUB set to inactive for Shift 1, making the opponent's HUB active.
- `SHIFT 2` (1:45 - 1:20): 25 seconds. HUBS swap active states.
- `SHIFT 3` (1:20 - 0:55): 25 seconds. HUBS swap active states.
- `SHIFT 4` (0:55 - 0:30): 25 seconds. HUBS swap active states.
- `END GAME` (0:30 - 0:00): 30 seconds. Both HUBS are active. Robots climb the TOWER.

FUEL scored in an inactive HUB earns 0 points. Verify exact timing, active-HUB rules, and tie handling in the manual before using these details in strategy or legality answers.

## Notable Robot Constraints

Verify current rule text and any Team Update changes before relying on these constraints:

- `R103`: max 115.0 lb robot weight, excluding battery and bumpers. `R408` sets a 135.0 lb absolute maximum with bumpers.
- `R104`: max 110.0 in frame perimeter, max 30.0 in height, and no overhang in starting configuration.
- `R105` and `R106`: max 12.0 in horizontal extension beyond the robot perimeter. Extension may only be over one side of the robot perimeter at a time.
- `R107`: total robot height may never exceed 30.0 in.
- `R502`: max 4 propulsion motors.
- `R401` through `R412`: bumper rules. BUMPERS must cover the perimeter with no gaps larger than 1.25 in. Bumper zone is 2.5 in to 5.75 in from the floor. Max extension from perimeter is 4.0 in.

## Key Gameplay Rules

Use these as search anchors, then quote the manual:

- `G403`: in AUTO, robots completely across the center line may not contact opponent robots.
- `G407`: robots may only launch FUEL into their HUB if their BUMPERS are partially or fully within their alliance zone.
- `G408`: robots may not gain greater than momentary control of FUEL released by HUB exits until it hits the ground or another robot.
- `G410` and `G413`: extension limits and field interaction limits. Interacting with carpet, BUMPS, or the TOWER BASE such that BUMPERS are lifted out of the bumper zone is a minor foul.
- `G418`: 3-second pin limit. A robot must separate by 72 in or move 72 in to reset the count.
- `G420`: during the last 30 seconds, contacting an opponent touching their TOWER is a major foul, and if the opponent is off the ground, they are awarded Level 3 TOWER points.
