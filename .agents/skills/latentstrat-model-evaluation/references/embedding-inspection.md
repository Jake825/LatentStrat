# Embedding Inspection

LatentStrat embeddings are learned team representations. Use them to inspect behavior, not as standalone proof.

## PCA Embeddings

PCA projections can show broad structure in learned embeddings. Interpret clusters alongside:

- Team data availability.
- Season and event coverage.
- Known strength or role context.
- Model metrics and calibration.

Do not over-explain a two-dimensional projection as if it fully represents the embedding space.

## Cosine Neighbors

Cosine neighbor reports help find teams with similar learned representations. Use them to generate hypotheses, then compare against raw data and scouting context.

## Archetypes

Archetype reports can summarize similarity to reference teams or behavior patterns. Keep archetype definitions explicit and avoid treating them as official labels.
